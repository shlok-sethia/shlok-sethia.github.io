Page Access Obliviousness Engine · Intel SGX Defence System

Researched and engineered a software solution to harden Intel i5-Skylake (and above) chips against side-channel and controlled-channel attacks — the class of exploits behind the 2017 Meltdown & Spectre catastrophe that rendered every x86 Intel CPU sold before 2017 vulnerable, costing the security industry US$200+ billion.

Built alongside a team of IISc PhD students and a leading professor from the Indian Institute of Science in Bangalore — India’s premier research and innovation institution — the work leveraged Page Access Obliviousness (PAO) with TLB-level prefetching in gem5 to obfuscate page access patterns attackers exploit through Intel SGX enclaves and eliminate the memory footprint side-channel entirely.
